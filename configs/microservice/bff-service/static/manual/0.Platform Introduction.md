# Platform Product Introduction

Yes is a one-stop, enterprise-level, commercially friendly AI development platform. We are dedicated to providing secure, efficient, and compliant AI solutions for enterprises. We adopt "technology openness and ecosystem cooperation" as our core principle, and through the integration of leading-edge models, business process automation, and other cutting-edge technologies, we have built a comprehensive Features system that covers the entire model lifecycle management, MCP, online retrieval, agent rapid development, enterprise Knowledge Base construction, and complex workflow arrangement. The platform adopts a modular architecture design, supports flexible Features expansion and second development, and greatly reduces the App barrier to entry for AI technology. Whether it is small and medium-sized enterprises quickly building intelligent apps or large enterprises implementing complex business transformation with AI, we can provide powerful technical support to help enterprises accelerate their digital transformation process and drive business innovation.



## Core Advantages

**License Free:** Built using the Apache 2.0 License, the platform provides a generous open ecosystem, eliminating the commercial permission restrictions, and granting enterprises full autonomy in expanding and developing.

**Enterprise-level multi-tenancy:** Provides multi-tenant account systems, meeting the requirements of data security and scalability, and solving the challenges faced by open-source platforms in multi-organization scenarios.

**High precision RAG:** Achieves significant improvements in performance from various dimensions, enhancing the generation effectiveness of models, support for multi-modal retrieval, the adaptation of cascaded segmentation algorithm enhances the recall matching degree of long text, and the adaptation of multi-route retrieval fusion algorithm enhances the retrieval diversity. The overall RAG precision is significantly higher than that of the mainstream open-source RAG methods.

**Native support for MCP:** Users need to install the Tools plugin to call the full-scale MCP Service. Standardized Interface protocols significantly reduce the cost of connecting Tools. The platform on MCP Square also integrates over 100 high-quality industry MCP Servers, facilitating quick calls by various industries.

**Native support for online retrieval:** Users can use the online retrieval Service by installing the Tools plugin when creating an Agent. The platform allows users to retrieve knowledge from over a billion web pages, covering weather, news, and encyclopedia, improving the response effectiveness of the Agent.

**Safety Guardrails:** Supports user-defined sensitive word tables, industry sensitive word tables, and real-time interception of high-risk content, ensuring the safety and compliance of content.



## Module Features

Model Management: Supports importing LLM, Embedding and Rerank models, and performing model inference.
Knowledge Base: Supports creating and managing Knowledge Base, performing knowledge management, and providing vector retrieval, full-text retrieval, and mixed retrieval.
MCP Square: Provides a curated recommendation of MCP servers, and supports importing custom MCP Service.
Safety Guardrails: Supports creating and managing sensitive word tables, controlling the safety of model feedback results, and protecting content safety and compliance.
Text QA: Supports creating and managing Text QAApp, and generating API Interface.
Workflow: Supports creating and managing Workflow, and performing debugging and publishing.
Agent: Supports creating and managing Agent, and performing debugging and publishing.
App Square: Provides App experience and historical App management Features.
Settings: Supports personal information modification, Organization management, Organization switching, and platform Configuration.



## Applicable Scenarios

**Intelligent customer service:** Provides intelligent dialogue, automatic replies, and other Features to improve customer service efficiency and service quality.
**Intelligent writing:** Assists in generating content, reports, etc., improving writing efficiency and quality.
**Intelligent data analysis:** Automatically analyzes data, generates insights, and supports decision-making.
**Business process automation:** Automates business processes, improving work efficiency.
