### 2. Historical Apps

You can view all historically used applications through historical apps.

Agents can view historical dialogue content, continue interacting and asking questions in the historical dialogue window, or start a new dialogue to use again.

For Text QA applications, historical dialogue content is not recorded, only online use is supported.
