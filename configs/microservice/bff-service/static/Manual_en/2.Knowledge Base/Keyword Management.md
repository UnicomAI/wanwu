# Keyword Management

In Knowledge Base Q&A scenarios, you can configure mapping rules between colloquial keywords used in questions and words in knowledge base documents to improve knowledge base content recall effectiveness and answer accuracy.

![image-20250725145521672](image-20250725145521672-1754471723897-7.png)

Click "Create Keywords", select associated knowledge base, and map colloquial expressions with literary words.
