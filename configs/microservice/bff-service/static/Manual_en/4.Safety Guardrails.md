# Safety Guardrails

According to different industry needs, users can maintain their own sensitive word lists to control the security of model feedback results.

### 1. Create Word List

Click "Safety Guardrails" to enter sensitive word management

![image-20250731115157547](image-20250731115157547.png)

Click "Create Sensitive Words" to enter the creation interface

![image-20250731115250930](image-20250731115250930.png)

### 2. Edit Word List

To edit the word list content, click the "Edit" button in the operation column of the sensitive word management list to enter the word list editing page.

Click "Add Sensitive Words" to add sensitive words individually or in batches.

![image-20250731115354503](image-20250731115354503.png)

![image-20250731115336465](image-20250731115336465.png)

![image-20250731115405666](image-20250731115405666.png)

Click "Reply Settings" to customize the model's reply content after sensitive words are triggered.

If not set, the system will reply by default:

Your current request or historical information contains sensitive content. According to relevant security requirements, this conversation round is terminated. Please clear historical information or start a new conversation.

![image-20250731120038928](image-20250731120038928.png)

![image-20250731120048044](image-20250731120048044.png)

### 3. Use Sensitive Words

Users can configure safety guardrails in agents or text QA applications and associate created sensitive word lists.

After enabling the safety guardrails tool, if sensitive words are triggered, responses will be provided according to the configured replies.
